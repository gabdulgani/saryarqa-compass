    4Arrive

    Arrive prepared, not puzzled.

    Central Asia can feel complicated from home. Pick your passport and the Arrival Concierge shows the entry rules with links to the official sources, flight options into Astana or Almaty, and a driver waiting with your name.

    How booking works.

    Four steps, all on this site. You always see the full price in euros before you pay anything.

    1. Choose a trip and date

      Pick a group departure or ask for a private date. The Trip Builder can suggest one.

    2. Meet your guide

      Request a guide you liked, or we match you with a verified one who speaks your language.

    3. Pay a 20% deposit

      By Visa or Mastercard, in euros. The rest is due 30 days before you travel.

    4. Get your arrival pack

      Entry rules, flight tips, your driver's details and a WhatsApp group with your guide.

    Questions we hear from Germany.

    Something missing? Ask the Trip Builder, or a manager on WhatsApp between 09:00 and 18:00 Astana time.

    What is included in the price?
    Your licensed guide, private transport, national park and entrance fees, the nights listed (twin rooms) and the meals shown for each day. Flights, travel insurance, tips and optional extras such as horse rides are not included and are listed with their price.
    Do I need a visa?
    Citizens of Germany, Austria and Switzerland can currently visit Kazakhstan without a visa for up to 30 days. Rules change, so the Arrival Concierge links you to the official sources to confirm before you fly.
    How fit do I need to be?
    Each day shows distance, ascent and maximum altitude. Most days have a gentle option, for example a lakeshore path instead of the climb to Kolsai Lake 2.
    What happens if my guide is ill?
    We replace them with another verified guide who speaks your language and tell you before the trip starts. If you'd rather not travel with someone else, you can cancel free of charge.
    When is the best time to go?
    Mountain lakes and canyons: mid-May to early October. Burabay: May to September. Turkestan: April to June and September to October, because July and August are very hot.

    How we build it in 12 weeks.

    Building all four features fully for every tour would take longer than 12 weeks, so the MVP is a smaller version made to answer one question: will a German traveler who has never heard of Saryarqa Routes trust the company enough to pay a deposit online?

    How we judge the pilot

    We set these targets ourselves; they don’t come from industry benchmarks. If testers still don’t trust the company or can’t pay, those problems are fixed before any money is spent on advertising.

    Team: two freelance web developers for the full 12 weeks, a designer for the first two months, a translator with a native German proofreader, and a photographer for the panoramas and guide videos. One company manager acts as product owner, because only the company knows the real prices, dates and inclusions.

    20,000,000 ₸first-year budget · team estimates

    From year 2, running costs are about 5,000,000 ₸ a year: 1.2M for hosting and tools, 1.8M for part-time maintenance and 2.0M for marketing.

    Ready-made tools, no custom technology

    Payment is the part we are least sure about. In weeks 1–2 the company compares the international card services of Kazakhstani banks with global providers that work there, and chooses by fees and by whether foreign Visa and Mastercard payments actually go through.
